Mediterranean Salmon Bowl

schedule 25 min
trending_up Easy
Protein 32g
Carbs 45g

Ingredients

Serves 2 people

Preparation

1
skillet

Cook the Salmon

Season salmon with olive oil, salt, and pepper. Sear in a hot pan for 4-5 minutes per side until golden brown and flaky.

2
restaurant_menu

Prep the Veggies

Halve the cherry tomatoes, slice the cucumber into thin moons, and finely chop fresh parsley for garnish.

3
layers

Assemble the Bowl

Start with a base of warm quinoa. Arrange the salmon, tomatoes, and cucumber. Top with a generous dollop of tzatziki sauce.
